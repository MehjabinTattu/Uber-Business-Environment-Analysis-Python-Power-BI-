# 🚗 Uber Business Environment Analysis (Python + Power BI)

## 🧠 Objective
Analyze Uber’s operational and business environment using data analytics to uncover trends in demand, pricing, and regional performance.  
Combine **Python-based analysis** with an interactive **Power BI dashboard** for strategic insights.

## ⚙️ Tools & Technologies
Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Power BI, SQL

## 🚀 Key Steps
- Collected and cleaned Uber trip and regional business data  
- Conducted **exploratory data analysis (EDA)** to identify key performance drivers  
- Analyzed trip demand patterns by **region, time, and weather conditions**  
- Examined relationships between pricing, wait times, and customer satisfaction  
- Developed **Power BI dashboards** to visualize KPIs such as trip count, average fare, and peak hours  
- Integrated geospatial visuals for mapping performance by region

## 📈 Results
- Identified top-performing regions by revenue and trip frequency  
- Detected demand surges during peak hours and weather-related fluctuations  
- Provided **data-driven recommendations** for surge pricing and driver allocation strategies  
- Delivered an **interactive Power BI dashboard** to monitor operational metrics
